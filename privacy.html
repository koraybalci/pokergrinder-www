PokerGrinder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

PokerGrinder is a poker session tracker for Android and iOS, run from New South Wales, Australia. This policy explains what the app and this website do with your information, in plain terms. "We" means PokerGrinder.

The short version

  • Your sessions, hands, results, venues and player notes are stored on your phone. We don't upload them to our servers.
  • You don't need an account to use the app.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads in the app.
  • We use a small number of service providers for sign-in, analytics, crash reports, feature settings and feedback. They are listed below.
  • If you choose "My location" in the Schedule, your location stays on your phone.

Information stored only on your phone

Everything you log in the app is kept in a database on your device. That includes sessions, buy-ins and results, hands, stack checks, venues, players and notes. We can't see it.

  • Phone backups: your phone's own backup (for example to your Google or Apple account) may include this data, so that you can move it to a new phone. That backup is controlled by you and your phone's provider.
  • Exports: if you export your data, the app creates a file and you choose where it goes.
  • Sharing: if you share a session, hand or image, it goes wherever you send it.

Information we collect

If you create an account

An account is optional. If you sign in with Google, Apple or an email code, we receive your email address and an account identifier from that sign-in. We use these only to run your account.

If you create a profile

A profile is optional and is stored on our servers so that it can be shared. It holds:

  • your display name, country, your profile link, your organisers, and your Hendon Mob link if you add one;
  • summary counts worked out from your sessions: sessions, tournaments, cash games, wins, cashes, final tables, your best finish, organisers played and time logged.

Your individual sessions and your money results are not part of your profile. Anyone who has your profile link can view your profile, so treat that link as public once you share it.

Automatically, when you use the app

  • App usage: which screens and features are used, with limited details such as session length or a bust reason, but not your buy-ins or results. This is linked to a random ID created when the app is first installed, not to your name.
  • App opens: one record per install per day, with that random ID, your platform, the app version, and your account ID if you are signed in. We use this to count installs.
  • Crash reports: technical details about errors, your device model and operating system, and the steps leading up to the error.
  • Install source: on Android, the Google Play referrer, and on iOS, Apple's ad attribution data, which tell us which link or campaign led to the install.
  • Home country and city: the home location you pick in the app, or a country taken from your phone's region until you pick one, is used with analytics and to switch on features for your area, such as the Sydney Schedule.
  • Technical identifiers: like any online service, our providers receive your IP address when the app connects to them, and may use it to work out your general region. On Android, our analytics provider may also use your device's advertising ID where your device makes it available.

If you send feedback

When you send feedback or report a profile, we receive what you write, any screenshot you choose to attach, your email address if you give it, and basic details about your device and app version.

Location

If your home location is Sydney, the filters on the Schedule page offer "My location". Only if you tap it and allow access, the app reads your device's approximate location to show how far each tournament is from you, and to filter and sort by that distance.

  • Your location is read only while you are on the Schedule page, never in the background.
  • It is kept in memory on your phone. It is not saved, is not sent to us or to any other company, and is not used for advertising or analytics.
  • Your on/off choice is saved on your phone. The app also saves public venue locations it looks up from tournament websites. Neither is included in data exports.
  • To stop, choose "Sydney CBD" in the Schedule filters, or remove the app's location permission in your phone's settings.

Tournament websites

The Schedule loads public listings directly from poker organisers' websites. Those requests come from your phone, so those sites can see your IP address, as with any website you visit. They do not include your location, your account or your poker data. Each site has its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use it to run the app and your account, show your profile, fix crashes, understand which features are used so that we can improve them, measure where installs come from, decide which cities to support next, and respond to you. We don't use it to send marketing messages.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We share it only with these providers, who process it to run the service:

  • Supabase: sign-in, accounts, profiles and app open records.
  • Google: Firebase Analytics for app usage, and Google sign-in if you use it.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple if you use it, and ad attribution on iOS.
  • Sentry: crash reports and performance data.
  • LaunchDarkly: turning features on and off, using a random or account ID, your platform, and your home country and city.
  • Wiredash: in-app feedback and app usage events.

We expect these providers to protect your information to at least the standard in this policy. Some are based outside Australia, so your information may be processed in other countries, including the United States. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or if PokerGrinder is ever sold or transferred, in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information until you are told otherwise.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete it. Settings has "Wipe local data", and uninstalling the app removes it too. Phone backups are kept under your backup provider's settings.
  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • App opens: kept for counting installs. Your account ID is removed from them when you delete your account.
  • Analytics, crash reports and feedback: kept only as long as they are useful, under the retention settings of each provider.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, open the Profile tab, open the account menu and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and profile from our servers, and your profile link stops working. Data on your phone stays until you wipe it. You can also email support@pokergrinder.app from your account's email address and ask us to delete it.
  • Permissions: you can remove location or notification permission at any time in your phone's settings. The app keeps working without them.
  • Access or correction: you can change your profile in the app, or email us to ask what we hold about you or to correct it.
  • Complaints: please contact us first. Depending on where you live you may have other rights, and you can complain to your local privacy regulator. In Australia that is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Security

Information sent to our providers is encrypted in transit, and your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

PokerGrinder is for adults and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

This website

Our website uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies, to measure visits and which links people arrive from. Public profile pages load the profile you are viewing from our servers. This policy page does not use analytics.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
